## The Three Protocols (In Plain Language)

### 1. ACP = Your Wardrobe's Communication System

**What it does:** Lets your wardrobe talk to helpful services

**Real-world example:**
```
Morning routine:
- Twin Style checks weather: "It's 65°F and cloudy today"
- Twin Style checks calendar: "You have a presentation at 2pm"
- Twin Style asks fashion AI: "What's appropriate?"
- Twin Style suggests: "Navy blazer + white shirt + dark jeans"
- You get dressed in 5 minutes, confident and appropriate
```

**Without ACP:** You check weather app, calendar app, guess at outfit, second-guess yourself, run late  
**With ACP:** Automatic suggestion considering all context, instant confidence

### 2. A2A = Your Apps Working Together

**What it does:** Different apps coordinate automatically

**Real-world example:**
```
Your calendar adds event: "Wedding on Saturday"
    ↓
Twin Style automatically:
- Checks your formal clothing
- Suggests outfit options
- Reminds you 3 days before
- Checks weather forecast for Saturday
- Adjusts suggestion if weather changes
```

**Without A2A:** You forget to plan, panic Friday night, make poor choices  
**With A2A:** Automatic planning, stress-free preparation

### 3. MCP = Your Wardrobe's Memory

**What it does:** Tracks everything you own and wear

**Real-world example:**
```
You think: "What haven't I worn lately?"
Twin Style shows:
- Gray sweater (not worn in 2 months)
- Blue dress (not worn in 3 weeks)
- Brown boots (not worn this season)

Twin Style suggests:
"How about gray sweater + black jeans today?
 You look great in it but rarely wear it!"
```

**Without MCP:** Wear same 5 items repeatedly, forget what you own  
**With MCP:** Balanced rotation, use your whole wardrobe
