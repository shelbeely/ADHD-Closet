## The Protocols Explained (Simple Version)

### ACP = Your Wardrobe's Phone Line

**What it does:** Lets your wardrobe talk to other helpful services

**Like this:**
- Twin Style calls Weather Service: "What's the forecast?"
- Twin Style calls Calendar: "What's on the schedule?"
- Twin Style calls Fashion AI: "Does this outfit work?"

**Result:** Your wardrobe has context, makes smart suggestions

---

### A2A = Apps Talking to Each Other

**What it does:** Different apps coordinate automatically

**Like this:**
- Calendar adds "Wedding Saturday" → Wardrobe starts planning formal outfit
- Weather alerts "Cold snap coming" → Wardrobe suggests layering options
- Photo app imports closet photo → Wardrobe adds new item automatically

**Result:** Everything works together seamlessly

---

### MCP = Your Wardrobe's Memory

**What it does:** Keeps track of everything you own and wear

**Like this:**
- "Where's my blue shirt?" → "Bottom drawer, worn 3 days ago"
- "What haven't I worn lately?" → "Gray sweater, 2 months"
- "Show me all black pants" → Instant list with photos

**Result:** Never forget what you own, always find what you need
